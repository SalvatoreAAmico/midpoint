## What Midpoint is

A tool that suggests places for a group to meet, ranked so that no one person
absorbs the whole journey. It is provided free.

## What it is not

**It does not verify that any suggested place exists, is open, is safe, or is
any good.** Suggestions come from OpenStreetMap, a public database anyone may
edit. Opening hours are frequently missing or out of date. Travel times are
estimates from a third-party routing service and take no account of traffic,
weather, closures or how you actually travel.

**Check anywhere before you go.** Use your own judgement about meeting people
and about where.

## Sharing location

Sharing a location is a choice you make each time, and you can decline and type
a neighbourhood instead.

**A session link is as sensitive as the locations in it.** Anyone holding it can
see where the people in that session are until it expires. Send it only to
people you intend to share that with, and do not post one publicly.

Do not share anyone else's location without their agreement.

## Acceptable use

Do not use Midpoint to track, follow, stalk or harass anyone; to break any law;
to attempt to reach data belonging to sessions you were not invited to; or to
place automated load on it or on the free services it depends on.

Supabase — which may be slow, rate-limited or unavailable, without notice. The
app may therefore stop working at any time, and no availability is promised.
